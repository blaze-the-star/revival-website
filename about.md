# About Revival

## The Story

Joseph wakes up in a forest he doesn't recognize. The ground beneath him is dirt instead of his bed, the air smells wild and unfamiliar, and when he looks into a bowl of water... he sees a fox staring back at him.

**Revival** is a Christian fantasy web serial about finding yourself when everything you knew has been stripped away. It's a story about:

- **Identity** - What defines us when our familiar world is gone?
- **Faith** - How do we hold onto truth in a place that doesn't make sense?
- **Belonging** - Where is home when you're far from everything you've known?
- **Transformation** - What happens when change isn't just external, but reaches deep within?

In a world of talking animals, ancient mysteries, and powers Joseph doesn't understand, he'll have to discover who he really is—and who he's meant to become.

---

## What Makes Revival Unique

This isn't your typical portal fantasy. Revival blends:

- **Christian themes** woven naturally into an epic fantasy setting
- **Anthropomorphic characters** with depth, culture, and meaningful relationships
- **Character-driven storytelling** that prioritizes internal growth alongside external adventure
- **Thoughtful exploration** of what it means to be transformed, body and soul
- **A web serial format** allowing the story to unfold week by week, building a journey we take together
