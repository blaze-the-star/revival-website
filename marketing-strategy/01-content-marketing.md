# Content Marketing Strategy

## Goal
Create valuable content that attracts readers, builds authority, and keeps your audience engaged between chapter releases.

## Content Pillars

### 1. Main Story Content (Priority 1)
- **New Chapters** - Your primary content
- **Publishing Schedule** - Aim for consistency (weekly, bi-weekly, or monthly)
- **Chapter Format** - Keep current clean format with clear navigation

**Action Items:**
- [ ] Establish a consistent publishing schedule
- [ ] Create a content calendar for upcoming chapters
- [ ] Add "Previous Chapter" navigation to all posts
- [ ] Include estimated reading time on each chapter

### 2. Behind-the-Scenes Content (Priority 2)
Readers love seeing the creative process!

**Content Ideas:**
- **Writing Process Posts** - Share your journey, challenges, victories
- **World-Building Articles** - Explain the world of Revival (geography, culture, history)
- **Character Profiles** - Deep dives into Joseph, Soot, Eli, Crystal, etc.
- **Deleted Scenes** - Scenes that didn't make the cut
- **Author Commentary** - Annotated chapters with your thoughts
- **Art & Visual Development** - If you commission any artwork
- **Chapter Retrospectives** - What you learned writing each arc

**Frequency:** 1-2 posts per month between chapters

**Action Items:**
- [ ] Create character profile templates
- [ ] Write world-building primer (setting, rules, culture)
- [ ] Share your writing process and tools
- [ ] Post "Why I wrote Revival" origin story

### 3. Reader Engagement Content (Priority 2)
Build community through interaction

**Content Ideas:**
- **Discussion Questions** - At end of each chapter
- **Fan Art Showcases** - Feature reader creations
- **Reader Theories** - Highlight interesting predictions
- **Q&A Sessions** - Answer reader questions monthly
- **Polls & Surveys** - Let readers vote on minor story elements
- **Reading Guides** - Themes, symbolism, character arcs
- **Chapter Recap Posts** - "Previously in Revival" summaries

**Action Items:**
- [ ] Add discussion questions to chapter endings
- [ ] Create fan art submission form
- [ ] Host monthly Q&A in newsletter
- [ ] Create "Story So Far" recap page

### 4. SEO & Discovery Content (Priority 3)
Content designed to bring new readers through search

**Blog Post Ideas:**
- "Best Free Fantasy Web Serials to Read in 2025"
- "Christian Fantasy Books Like [Popular Title]"
- "What is the Isekai Genre? A Guide for Fantasy Fans"
- "How to Read Revival: A Beginner's Guide"
- "Top Anthropomorphic Fantasy Stories"
- "Faith-Based Fantasy: Where to Start"

**Action Items:**
- [ ] Research keyword opportunities in fantasy fiction niche
- [ ] Write 3-5 SEO-optimized blog posts
- [ ] Create comprehensive "New Reader's Guide"
- [ ] Write comparison posts to popular series

### 5. Email Newsletter Content
Your most direct line to readers

**Newsletter Components:**
- **New Chapter Announcements** - Primary purpose
- **Personal Updates** - Connect authentically
- **Exclusive Content** - Bonus scenes, early previews
- **Reader Spotlights** - Feature engaged community members
- **Behind-the-Scenes** - Writing progress updates
- **Recommendations** - Books/media you're enjoying

**Frequency:**
- Minimum: Every new chapter
- Optimal: Weekly or bi-weekly digest

**Action Items:**
- [ ] Set up email automation for new chapters
- [ ] Create welcome email sequence (5 emails)
- [ ] Write monthly "State of Revival" update template
- [ ] Offer exclusive content for subscribers

## Content Calendar Template

### Monthly Rhythm
- **Week 1:** New chapter release + newsletter
- **Week 2:** Behind-the-scenes or world-building post
- **Week 3:** Reader engagement content or character profile
- **Week 4:** SEO content or author update

### Seasonal Content
- **Quarterly:** Major story arc recaps
- **Annually:** "Year in Review" and "What's Coming"

## Writing Style Guide
To maintain consistency across all content:

- **Tone:** Warm, authentic, passionate about storytelling
- **Voice:** Conversational but professional; enthusiastic but not pushy
- **Perspective:** Share both triumphs and struggles
- **Engagement:** Ask questions, invite responses
- **Faith Integration:** Natural, not preachy; let themes speak through story

## Content Repurposing Strategy
Maximize every piece of content:

1. **Blog Post** → Social media snippets (5-7 posts)
2. **Character Profile** → Email newsletter feature → Social graphics
3. **World-Building** → Reddit/forum discussion starters
4. **Chapter** → Key quotes as social images
5. **Writing Process** → Thread/carousel on social media

## Tools & Resources
- **Content Calendar:** Notion, Trello, or Google Sheets
- **Graphics:** Canva (free) for quote cards and headers
- **Email:** Already using tr.ee link (assuming Beehiiv or similar)
- **SEO:** Ubersuggest, AnswerThePublic for keyword ideas
- **Analytics:** Google Analytics for your website
