# Platform Distribution Strategy

## Goal
Maximize Revival's reach by publishing on multiple platforms where web serial readers actively discover and consume fiction.

## Platform Overview

Publishing Revival on multiple platforms:
- **Increases discoverability** through platform algorithms and communities
- **Reduces risk** (not dependent on single platform)
- **Reaches different audiences** (each platform has unique demographics)
- **Builds credibility** (multi-platform presence appears established)
- **Provides backup** (content preserved across multiple sites)

## Recommended Platforms

### Tier 1: Essential Platforms

#### 1. **Your Own Website** (blog.revival.us.kg)
**Current Status:** ✅ Established
**Priority:** MAINTAIN

**Advantages:**
- Full ownership and control
- No platform restrictions
- Direct email list building
- Professional presence
- SEO benefits

**Optimization:**

- [ ] Create sitemap for SEO

**When readers should use this:**
"Your home base for the best reading experience and latest updates"

#### 2. **Royal Road**
**Priority:** HIGH - Start immediately
**Audience:** 5M+ monthly readers focused on web serials

**Why Royal Road?**
- Largest English web serial platform
- Active discovery through rankings and recommendations
- Strong community engagement (comments, reviews, follows)
- Built-in reader base looking for serialized fiction
- Good for fantasy/isekai genre
- Free to use

**Strategy:**
- Cross-post all existing chapters (release on schedule to build momentum)
- Post new chapters simultaneously with your site (or 1-2 days after for site priority)
- Engage with comments actively
- Use tags wisely: fantasy, isekai, christian, anthropomorphic, adventure
- Create compelling cover image
- Write strong synopsis (first impression is critical)
- Ask engaged readers for reviews (after 10+ chapters read)

**Setup Checklist:**
- [ ] Create author account
- [ ] Write compelling synopsis (hook in first 2-3 sentences)
- [ ] Design or commission cover image (minimum 400x600px)
- [ ] Set up fiction page with tags and metadata
- [ ] Schedule chapter releases (3-5 chapters per week to start, then match writing pace)
- [ ] Add author note directing to main website for email signup
- [ ] Link to your website in profile and fiction description

**Monetization:**
- Consider Patreon integration for advanced chapters (when ready)
- Royal Road also has optional ads and premium features

---

#### 3. **Scribble Hub**
**Priority:** MEDIUM-HIGH
**Audience:** 1M+ monthly readers, overlaps with Royal Road but distinct community

**Why Scribble Hub?**
- Second-largest web serial platform
- More lenient content policies
- Strong community features
- Good for non-traditional narratives
- Cross-promotion with Royal Road readers

**Strategy:**
- Similar to Royal Road approach
- Can use same cover and synopsis
- Engage with community forums
- Participate in recommendation threads

**When to Start:** After establishing Royal Road presence (1-2 months)

### Tier 2: Consider Adding

#### 4. **Wattpad**
**Priority:** MEDIUM
**Audience:** 90M+ users, younger demographic (13-24 primary)

**Why Wattpad?**
- Massive user base
- Strong mobile app experience
- Good for building young adult audience
- Active community features

**Cautions:**
- Audience skews younger
- Different reading culture (more YA romance-focused)
- May not be ideal fit for Christian fantasy
- Lower comment quality sometimes

**Strategy:**
- Test with first few chapters
- Monitor engagement
- Continue if resonates with audience

**When to Start:** 3-6 months after Royal Road

#### 5. **Tapas**
**Priority:** MEDIUM
**Audience:** Mobile-first readers, tipping culture

**Why Tapas?**
- Mobile-optimized reading
- Tipping feature (monetization)
- Good community
- Serialized format fits naturally

**Strategy:**
- Format chapters for mobile reading
- Release on consistent schedule
- Engage with comment section
- Eventually enable tipping

**When to Start:** 6+ months, when you have consistent output

#### 6. **Archive of Our Own (AO3)**
**Priority:** LOW-MEDIUM
**Audience:** Primarily fanfiction, but accepts original works

**Why AO3?**
- Excellent tagging system
- Engaged readership
- Strong search features
- Free and ad-free

**Cautions:**

- Original works less prominent

**Strategy:**

- Mark as original work
- Monitor engagement

**When to Start:** Optional; if you have bandwidth after other platforms

### Tier 3: Future Expansion

#### 7. **Patreon**
**Priority:** Consider after building audience
**Purpose:** Monetization, not distribution

**What to Offer:**
- Early chapter access (1-3 chapters ahead)
- Exclusive bonus content (deleted scenes, character profiles)
- Monthly Q&As
- Vote on minor story elements
- Behind-the-scenes content

**Tiers to Consider:**
- $3/month: Early access (1-2 chapters)
- $5/month: Early access (3+ chapters) + bonus content
- $10/month: All above + monthly Q&A + Discord role
- $25/month: All above + character cameo/name in story

**When to Start:** When you have 500+ engaged readers and consistent publishing schedule

## Multi-Platform Management

### Publishing Workflow

**Step 1: Write Chapter**
- Complete and edit on your system

**Step 2: Primary Release**
- Publish on your website FIRST
- Send email newsletter announcement

**Step 3: Platform Distribution** (same day or next day)
- Post to Royal Road
- Post to Scribble Hub (when added)
- Update other platforms

**Step 4: Promotion**
- Share on Reddit
- Tweet announcement
- Respond to comments on all platforms

### Tools for Management

**Cross-Posting:**
- Manually copy/paste (best quality control)
- Use formatting tools to maintain consistency
- Keep formatting simple (works across all platforms)

**Schedule Tracking:**
- Spreadsheet with posting checklist
- Track which chapters posted where
- Note engagement metrics per platform

**Response Management:**
- Check all platforms daily for first week after chapter
- Respond to comments within 24-48 hours
- Set notifications for engagement

### Content Adaptation by Platform

**All Platforms:**
- Maintain same story content
- Adjust author notes per platform culture
- Include links to email list (where allowed)

**Platform-Specific Notes:**
- **Your site:** More personal, direct
- **Royal Road:** Engage with review/rating culture
- **Wattpad:** Shorter author notes, emojis okay
- **Scribble Hub:** Can be more casual

## Platform-Specific SEO

### Royal Road Optimization
- **Tags:** Use all available relevant tags
- **Title:** Consider searchability (keep "Revival" but may add subtitle)
- **Synopsis:** Front-load hooks, include genre keywords
- **Update frequency:** Consistent schedule boosts rankings
- **Engagement:** More comments/reviews = better visibility

### Your Website SEO
- **Chapter titles:** Descriptive and keyword-rich
- **Meta descriptions:** Unique per chapter
- **Internal linking:** Link between chapters, to character pages
- **Alt text:** For any images
- **Schema markup:** Consider adding Book schema
- **Loading speed:** Optimize images and code

## Common Pitfalls to Avoid

❌ **Spreading too thin:** Start with 1-2 platforms, expand gradually
❌ **Inconsistent posting:** Pick schedule you can maintain
❌ **Ignoring comments:** Engagement builds community
❌ **Platform-exclusive:** Maintain your own site as primary
❌ **Same author note everywhere:** Customize per platform
❌ **Neglecting quality:** Don't rush posts for schedule
❌ **No cross-linking:** Direct readers to email list
❌ **Comparing numbers:** Each platform has different scale
