# Email Marketing Strategy

## Why Email is Your Most Important Channel

Email is the **only channel you truly own**. Social media platforms can change algorithms, ban accounts, or shut down. Email subscribers are YOUR audience, and you can reach them directly anytime.

**Benefits:**
- 🎯 Direct access to your audience
- 💰 Highest ROI of any marketing channel
- 📊 Measurable results
- 🔗 You own the relationship
- 🎁 Can segment and personalize
- 🚀 Drives most engaged readers

## Goals

**Year 1:**
- Month 3: 50 subscribers
- Month 6: 150 subscribers
- Month 12: 500 subscribers

**Year 2:**
- 1,000-2,000 subscribers

**Metrics:**
- Open rate: 35-45% (good for author newsletters)
- Click rate: 5-10%
- List growth: 10-20% monthly in first year

## Email Types & Sequences

### 1. Welcome Sequence (Automated)

**Purpose:** Hook new subscribers immediately

**Email 1: Welcome (Immediate)**
- Subject: "Welcome to Revival! Here's what happens next..."
- Thank them for subscribing
- Deliver lead magnet if applicable
- Link to start reading (Chapter 1.1)
- Set expectations (how often you email)
- Personal touch (who you are, why you write)

**Email 2: Origin Story (Day 2)**
- Subject: "Why I'm writing Revival (and why it matters)"
- Share your "why" – why this story, why free, why now
- Connect emotionally
- Invite reply/dialogue

**Email 3: Where to Start (Day 4)**
- Subject: "New to Revival? Start here"
- Reading guide
- Chapter highlights
- What to expect
- Community invitation (Discord, Reddit, etc.)

**Email 4: Behind the Scenes (Day 7)**
- Subject: "What it's really like writing Revival"
- Writing process peek
- Challenges and victories
- Character development insights
- Build connection

**Email 5: Engagement Prompt (Day 10)**
- Subject: "Quick question for you..."
- Ask about their reading experience
- Favorite character/moment so far
- What they want to see more of
- Encourage reply

**Action Items:**
- [ ] Write all 5 welcome emails
- [ ] Set up automation in email platform
- [ ] Test sequence yourself
- [ ] Monitor open/click rates
- [ ] Adjust based on performance

### 2. Chapter Release Emails (Regular)

**Purpose:** Notify subscribers of new content

**Template:**

**Subject Lines (Rotate):**
- "Revival [Chapter Number]: [Intriguing Chapter Title]"
- "New Chapter: [Something Exciting Happens]"
- "[Character Name]'s Choice: New Revival Chapter"
- "You won't believe what happens in Ch. [X]"

**Body Structure:**
1. **Hook:** One-line teaser or cliffhanger from previous chapter
2. **Announcement:** New chapter is live
3. **Preview:** 2-3 sentence chapter summary (no spoilers)
4. **CTA Button:** "Read Now"
5. **Personal Note:** Your writing update, insight, or ask
6. **Reminder:** How often you publish, what's coming

**Example:**
```
Joseph thought he could hide his secret forever.
He was wrong.

Chapter 1.16 is now live, and everything changes.

In this chapter, Joseph faces a choice that will alter his journey in Revival's world forever—and the consequences are closer than he thinks.

[Read Chapter 1.16 Now]

Writing this chapter was intense. I rewrote the ending three times before it felt right. Joseph's character arc has been building to this moment since Chapter 1, and I can't wait to hear what you think.

Reply to this email with your theories—I read every response!

Next chapter drops next Friday. Things are about to get wild.

- Blaze
```

**Frequency:** Every new chapter release

### 5. Re-engagement Campaign (Automated)

**Purpose:** Win back inactive subscribers

**Trigger:** No open in 30-60 days

**Email 1: "Are you still reading?"**
- Subject: "I haven't seen you in a while..."
- Miss them
- What's new in Revival
- Recap recent chapters
- Easy path back (link to continue reading)

**Email 2: "What happened?"**
- Subject: "Before you go..."
- Survey about why they stopped
- Offer to adjust email frequency
- Last chance to re-engage

**Email 3: "Goodbye for now"**
- Subject: "It's not you, it's me"
- Graceful goodbye
- Option to resubscribe anytime
- Remove from active list

## Email Best Practices

### Subject Lines
✅ **Do:**
- Keep under 50 characters
- Create curiosity without clickbait
- Use personalization when appropriate
- A/B test different approaches
- Preview text as extension

❌ **Don't:**
- Use ALL CAPS or excessive punctuation!!!
- Mislead or use clickbait
- Be vague or boring
- Start with "Newsletter #47"

### Email Body
✅ **Do:**
- Write conversationally (like to a friend)
- Keep paragraphs short (2-3 lines)
- Use one clear CTA per email
- Include images sparingly
- Make links obvious
- Encourage replies
- Sign with your name

❌ **Don't:**
- Write walls of text
- Include too many links
- Use attachments (deliverability issue)
- Forget mobile optimization
- Sound corporate or stiff

### Sending Strategy
- **Best Days:** Tuesday-Thursday (generally highest open rates)
- **Best Times:**
  - 9-11 AM (morning coffee reading)
  - 1-3 PM (lunch break)
  - 8-10 PM (evening relaxation)
- **Test for your audience:** Track open times, adjust schedule

## Segmentation Strategy

**Start Simple:** Everyone gets same emails

**As You Grow:** Segment by:
- **Engagement Level:** Active vs. inactive readers
- **Reading Progress:** New vs. caught-up readers
- **Platform:** Where they found you
- **Interests:** What content they engage with most

**Advanced Segmentation:**
- Super fans (open everything, always click)
- Beta readers (offer early access)
- Potential reviewers (ask for reviews/testimonials)
- Patreon candidates (highest engagement)

## Metrics & Optimization

### Key Metrics

**List Growth Rate:**
- Formula: (New Subscribers - Unsubscribes) / Total Subscribers × 100
- Target: 5-15% monthly growth in first year

**Open Rate:**
- Target: 35-45%
- Factors: Subject line, send time, sender name, frequency

**Click Rate:**
- Target: 5-10%
- Factors: Content relevance, CTA clarity, link placement

**Unsubscribe Rate:**
- Target: <0.5% per email
- Warning sign: >2%

**Conversion Rate:**
- What % complete action (read chapter, leave review, etc.)

### A/B Testing Ideas
- Subject line variations
- Send time/day
- Email length (short vs. detailed)
- CTA wording
- Personal vs. formal tone
- With/without images

### Monthly Review Process
- [ ] Check growth rate
- [ ] Review open/click rates
- [ ] Identify top-performing emails
- [ ] Note what subjects worked best
- [ ] Test one new variable
- [ ] Adjust strategy based on data
