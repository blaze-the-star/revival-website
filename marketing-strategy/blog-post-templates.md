Start with the moment that sparked Revival. What inspired this story?

**Example opening:**

[Write what makes Revival unique]

[Write about the heart of the story]

5. **Marketing Is About Connection, Not Promotion**

6. **The Writing Community Is Gold**

### Newsletter Best Practices:

**Subject Line Ideas:**
- "Revival Chapter [X] is live! 🦊"
- "Behind the scenes: [interesting tidbit]"
- "[Exciting update] + new chapter"
- "What I learned writing [character name]"

- Bi-weekly if publishing less frequently
- At minimum, monthly to stay connected

**Length:**
- Keep to 400-600 words
- Make it scannable (short paragraphs, bold headings)
- Mobile-friendly formatting

**Tone:**
- Conversational and warm
- Like writing to friends
- Authentic and personal
- Enthusiastic but not salesy

## Notes for Using These Templates

1. **Authenticity First:** Templates provide structure, but your voice makes them work. Write like you're talking to friends.

2. **Don't Force It:** If something feels unnatural, adjust or skip it. Marketing should energize, not drain you.

3. **Start Small:** You don't need to create all content at once. Focus on 2-3 pieces to begin.

4. **Iterate:** First drafts don't need to be perfect. Publish, learn, improve.

5. **Your Story Is The Best Marketing:** Great chapters will always outperform perfect marketing copy. Write well first.
