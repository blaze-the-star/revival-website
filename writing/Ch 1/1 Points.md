Joseph was sound asleep, snuggled under his cover when he was stirred.
He heard voices around him - urgent and unfamiliar.

> "We found him in *Chloros Forest*," one said.

> "Who just sleeps in a place like that?" another replied in disbelief.

Joseph listened, but his mind was slow to start and he couldn't surmise why they were talking weird.

%% Joseph's dialogue should be in modern English, not Revival English. It does not need to be changed. %%
> _Am I having a stroke?_ He thought.

That was when he realized, he wasn't sleeping in his bed.
He was outside in the dirt.

How'd he get here?

His eyes blinked open as he lifted his head in a daze.

> "Look, he's waking up." said another voice, distinctly feminine.

Joseph tried looking around, but the sun shone in his face and his eyes blurred.

He didn't catch much before a clay bowl of water was pushed toward him.

> "Here. We got you some water," the voice friendly and young.

Joseph didn't think. He lowered his head to the water, but then stopped.
His reflection wasn't right. Instead of his face, he saw a dark silhouette of pointed ears and a snout.

> _What is that?..._

He looked at it for a good few seconds, confused.
Then it dawned on him.

> "...Oh no..." His dead voice mumbled.
> "I'm a furry."

> "Huh?" said the one who gave him the water.

Joseph looked up at him. He saw brown baggy pants, a simple but long olive-green shirt with buttons and long sleeves and... an otter's face?
